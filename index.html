How to play

Learn in 10 seconds, play forever

Pick your side

One player is X, the other is O. X moves first.

Take turns

Tap an empty square to place your mark, then hand the phone to your friend.

Get three in a row

Line up three marks across, down or diagonally to win. A full board with no line is a draw.
